Why it's called TraceLayer

There's a trace layer under every task.

Every task runs as steps, and the trace layer tracks each one — what went in, what came out, whether it passed. When something goes wrong, we know exactly which step, send the fix back to that step alone, and get smarter about it.

Every hand-off between steps is a defined input and output. That's what makes errors findable — and improvements permanent.
