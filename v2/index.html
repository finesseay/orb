What it does

Every task is a conversation — you, your team, and your agents in one thread.

Talk & screenshare

Companion on your phone or desktop. Show your screen, say what you want — that becomes a task.

Durable memory

Your preferences and project facts stay current from one conversation to the next — and stay yours.

Always-on platform agents

Monitoring, fixing, and improving between your visits. The work does not wait for you to log in.

Not married to any model. Neither is your knowledge.

TraceLayer runs every major AI vendor, picking the right model for each step by cost and quality. Want to run a local model? Switch away from one you’ve stopped trusting? No problem. And your knowledge stays your knowledge — it moves with you, never with the model.

Feels like magic. It isn’t.

Underneath, the platform does the unglamorous work: durable knowledge that stays current as things change, dedicated agents watching performance and errors, every issue logged and fed back in. Results improve week after week — without anyone maintaining prompts.
